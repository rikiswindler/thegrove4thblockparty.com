About the Party

A neighborhood tradition unlike any other.

Every 4th of July, the Valmonte Grove comes together in the cul-de-sac for food, fun, and good company. This year we’re also celebrating a milestone: 250 years of America. Come ready to eat, play, and make memories with your neighbors.
